Why Seismic Reinforcement

Most wood-frame homes weren't built for today's standard

Seismic codes have changed more than once. A home that was fully compliant when it was built can still be significantly under-strength today — and from the outside, there's usually no way to tell.

The Data

Over 90% of pre-2000 wood-frame homes fall short

Of roughly 30,000 wood-frame homes built before May 2000 that underwent a formal seismic diagnosis, more than 9 in 10 were found structurally insufficient — nearly three-quarters rated likely to collapse in a major earthquake.
